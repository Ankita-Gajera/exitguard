ExitGuard console

A personal tripwire for vault deposits. A timelock exists so that depositors can leave before a change lands, but nobody watches one around the clock. ExitGuard does, and leaves for you.

Who can change your vault?

Paste the address of a vault you use. The scan reads who can change it, how much warning you would get, and which tripwires ExitGuard would lay. It only reads, and needs no wallet.

Try ExitGuard on testnets

Deploy your own ExitGuard, run a health check, and play either trigger: the protocol queues an upgrade, or its deployment on another chain is paused. Both times your deposit leaves first.

Use a throwaway testnet account. Nothing here needs real funds.
